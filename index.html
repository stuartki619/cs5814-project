01 / Overview

The project in brief

Large language models can produce false statements either because they are inaccurate or because their outputs conflict with information they internally represent as true. This distinction motivates the need for methods that improve model honesty without degrading general capabilities. In this work, we investigate steering as a mechanism for reducing dishonest behavior through interventions applied at different stages of the language-model pipeline. We systematically evaluate and ablate input controls, structural controls, state controls, and output controls across different models and various public benchmarks, detailed later in the paper.
